A lettings practice, not a lettings factory.

Yarmside is a small, independent practice on Yarm High Street. We let and manage homes across the Tees Valley for landlords who want their property treated like the serious investment it is.

How we work

The lettings industry drifted somewhere odd. Portfolios got huge, offices got quiet, and the person managing your property became a ticket number in someone's inbox. We think a property deserves better than that, and so does the person living in it.

So we keep the practice small on purpose. Every home on our books has been visited, photographed and described by one of us in person. Every tenant has sat across a table from us. Every landlord knows who picks up the phone, because it's always one of the same few people.

The other thing we care about is plain dealing. Fees quoted in writing before you commit. Statements on the same day every month. If we think a property will struggle to let at the price you have in mind, we'll tell you at the valuation, not three quiet weeks into marketing.

The name comes from where we work. Yarm's High Street has been a trading street for centuries, and the orange in our logo is taken from its Victorian brickwork. We like being from somewhere, and we intend to still be here, on the same street, in twenty years.

Four rules we don't bend.

Rule one

See it before you say it

No home is described, valued or listed by someone who hasn't stood in it. Templates don't visit properties, we do.

Rule two

Bad news travels fast

If rent is late, a boiler has failed or a tenancy is wobbling, you hear it from us the day we know, with a plan attached.

Rule three

Tenants are clients too

A well-treated tenant stays longer, pays reliably and looks after the house. Respect for tenants isn't a cost, it's the strategy.

Rule four

The statement is sacred

Same day, every month, one page you can actually read. If you've ever chased an agent for your own money, you know why this matters.
