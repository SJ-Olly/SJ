Your property, looked after like it's ours.

We're buy-to-let specialists. Most of our landlords own one or two properties and want them let well, kept compliant and paid on time, without having to think about it every week. That's the job, and we do all of it from the office on the High Street.

Choose how much you want to hand over.

Every service starts with the same free valuation. You can move between levels later — plenty of our fully managed landlords started with tenant find only.

Tenant find

For landlords who manage day to day themselves and just want the right tenant, found properly.

  • Valuation and rental appraisal
  • Photography and listing on Rightmove and OnTheMarket
  • Accompanied viewings
  • Full referencing, right to rent checks and credit checks
  • Tenancy agreement and deposit registration

Fee: one-off, quoted at valuation. We publish a plain fee sheet, ask us for it.

Fully managed

Our main service. Most landlords who join us choose this, especially those who don't live locally.

  • Everything in tenant find
  • Rent collection and arrears handling
  • Repairs coordinated with trusted local trades
  • Quarterly inspections with a written note to you
  • Compliance tracked and renewed before it lapses
  • One clear statement, issued the same day every month

Fee: a percentage of monthly rent, quoted at valuation. No set-up fee, no hidden extras, no mark-up on contractor invoices.

Rent collection

For landlords who handle repairs themselves but want the money side run properly.

  • Everything in tenant find
  • Rent collected by standing order
  • Arrears chased early and courteously
  • Monthly statement on the same day each month
  • Annual income summary for your tax return

Fee: a smaller percentage of monthly rent, quoted at valuation.

We don't publish headline percentages here because they depend on the property and what it needs. What we will promise: the quote you get at valuation is the fee you pay, in writing, before you commit to anything.

From valuation to move-in, in four stages.

Always in this order, coordinated end to end from the office on the High Street.

01

Valuation

A face-to-face visit, an honest rental appraisal, and a recommendation on positioning — backed by twenty-two years of comparables. If we think you'd do better selling, or waiting, we'll say so.

02

Preparation

Photography, descriptions, compliance checks (EICR, gas, EPC, smoke alarms) and any pre-tenancy works — done first so nothing holds the tenancy up later.

03

Tenant find

Marketed on Rightmove, OnTheMarket and our own list. Tenants referenced through HomeLet, vetted in person, matched to the property before we recommend them to you.

04

Management

Rent collection, quarterly inspections, repair coordination, statement issued on the same day — every month, without fail.

The rules keep changing. Keeping up is our job.

Private renting is one of the most regulated things an ordinary person can do with their money. For managed properties we track every requirement and renew it before it lapses.

Electrical safety EICR renewed every five years

Gas safety CP12 certificate renewed annually

Energy performance EPC in place and re-run when it expires

Alarms Smoke and CO alarms checked at every inspection

Deposits Protected in a government-approved scheme

Right to rent Checked for every adult occupant
