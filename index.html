How a tenancy runs

From valuation to move-in, in four stages, always in this order.

01 · Valuation

A face to face visit, an honest read of the market, and a fee-free recommendation on price, backed by real comparables on your street.

02 · Preparation

Photography, a proper description, and the compliance work, EICR, gas safety, EPC and smoke alarms, done first so nothing holds up marketing later.

03 · Tenant find

Listed on Rightmove and OnTheMarket, and matched against our own list of registered tenants. Every applicant referenced and met in person.

04 · Management

Rent collected, repairs coordinated, and one statement issued on the same day every month, so you always know where things stand.
