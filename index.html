What renting should be like

01

Valuation

A face-to-face visit, an honest rental appraisal, and a recommendation on positioning — backed by twenty-two years of comparables.

02

Preparation

Photography, descriptions, compliance checks (EICR, gas, EPC, smoke alarms) and any pre-tenancy works — coordinated end to end.

03

Tenant find

Marketed on Rightmove, OnTheMarket and our own list. Tenants referenced through HomeLet, vetted in person, matched to the property.

Management

Rent collection, quarterly inspections, repair coordination, statement issued on the same day — every month, without fail.
